About

I'm a quantitative finance professional with a background in Control and Automation Engineering and a specialization in fuzzy-logic-based machine learning and data science.

I grew up in Manaus, Amazonas, the capital of the Brazilian Amazon. That environment shaped how I think: complex systems, limited information, and decisions that still have to be made.

My undergraduate thesis at the State University of Amazonas introduced an interpretable evolving fuzzy model for time series prediction in financial markets, presented at the Future Alpha 2026 Data-Driven Markets Summit in New York City.

I'm completing my M.S. in Quantitative Finance at Northeastern University's D'Amore-McKim School of Business, graduating December 2026, and currently serving as Sector Manager at the 360 Huntington Fund (~$2M AUM). I'm actively targeting roles in portfolio management, quantitative research, trading, and risk analysis. Available full-time January 2027.

Outside of work, I make Neapolitan pizzas from scratch. It's one of those things I genuinely enjoy for its own sake, from mixing the dough to pulling it out of the oven. More than anything, it's an excuse to get good people around a table. Beach volleyball usually takes up the rest of my free time.
